# Zenith Robotics — Internal Notes

Zenith Robotics was founded in 2019 in Pune by Meera Kulkarni and Arjun Rao.

The company's first product, the ZR-100 warehouse robot, launched in March 2021.
It can lift 250 kg and runs for 14 hours on a single charge.

In 2023 the company opened a second office in Bengaluru with 85 employees.
Revenue in fiscal year 2024 was 42 crore rupees, a 60% increase over 2023.

The ZR-200, launched in January 2025, added computer vision for damaged-package
detection and reduced sorting errors by 38%.

Zenith's biggest customer is FreshKart, which operates 12 warehouses using
a fleet of 340 Zenith robots.
